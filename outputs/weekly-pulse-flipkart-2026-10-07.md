> Input: [`data/flipkart-play-store-reviews.csv`](../data/flipkart-play-store-reviews.csv)

## Top themes

- Many users report a generally positive and satisfying shopping experience.
- Recurring issues with delivery delays, lack of communication from delivery staff, and cancellations.
- Customer support via chatbot is often unhelpful, with no direct access to a human agent for urgent issues.

## What users are saying

> "i want to give you one Star only because Lately, you've started charging unnecessary extra fees that make no sense; you inflate the bill by adding platform fees, additional charges, and who knows what else."

> "Extremely frustrating experience with Flipkart! My product has been delayed, and there is no clear or reliable information about when it will actually be delivered. The worst part is the complete lack of proper customer support. There is no option to directly talk to a customer-care executive—only a chatbot that does not understand the issue and keeps giving irrelevant, repetitive responses."

> "Extremely disappointing experience. I placed an order twice, but received no call from the delivery executive. Even after a replacement order was issued, it got automatically canceled on its own. The delivery process is not smooth at all and causes a lot of frustration. Strict action should be taken to fix this broken system."

## Key observation

While many customers are happy overall, unresolved logistics issues (especially delivery delays and inconsistent support responses) are damaging trust, and confusion about extra fees is driving customer dissatisfaction.

## Action ideas for the product team

1. Increase transparency by clearly itemizing all platform fees and charges at checkout and providing tooltips to explain each cost.
2. Improve the delivery communication system by mandating delivery executives to contact customers and proactively update status changes or delays.
3. Enable an option for customers to escalate issues to a live human support agent when automated responses fail or delivery problems occur.

# Fee Explainer

**Identified fee issue:** platform fees, additional charges, and cancellation/return fees. Customers are frustrated by unclear or unexpected extra charges at checkout or for return/cancellation.

**Review signal:** Users report being charged extra or hidden fees such as platform fees, additional charges, or cancellation/return fees that are not clearly communicated or expected.

**Explanation:**

- No official Flipkart Help Center or FAQ page clearly defines a 'platform fee' charged to buyers at checkout.
- Official Terms of Use and seller Fee & Commission documentation focus on seller-side fees (e.g. fixed/closing fee, commission, collection and shipping fees) and do not address any buyer platform or handling charges.
- There is no documented official cancellation fee or return fee for buyers in Flipkart's publicly available Help Centre or policy pages.
- Therefore, the amount, timing, or conditions under which these buyer-facing fees would apply are not specified in official Flipkart documentation.
- Customers should be advised that Flipkart does not explicitly describe these buyer fees in its official buyer-facing policy documents.
- Support agents may need to escalate specific fee queries to internal policy teams if customers request clarifications.
